How progress is calculated

overall = Σ (milestone weight × implemented tasks / milestone tasks)

Every task within a milestone receives an equal share of that milestone's weight. Weights sum to 100%. A task counts when its executable behavior is implemented. Validation is tracked separately. This is a scope estimate, not a time estimate or an audio-quality score.
